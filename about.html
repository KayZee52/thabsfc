OUR HISTORY

Founded in 1968 in Monrovia, our club grew from weekend community games to a respected contender in Liberia’s top leagues. We’ve earned county titles, nurtured young talent, and inspired fans with a spirit of unity and determination.

UNITY

We stand together as one club, one family. Players, coaches, and supporters move with a single purpose—lifting each other on and off the pitch. Our strength is in the bonds we share, no matter the challenge

DETERMINATION

Every match is a test of heart. From first whistle to final minute, we fight for every ball and never back down. Setbacks fuel our drive to train harder and come back stronger.

FOCUS

Focus and preparation make champions. We honor the game through respect, smart tactics, and relentless practice, proving that consistent effort beats talent when talent doesn’t work hard.

COMMUNITY

Our club belongs to the people. We mentor youth, host local clinics, and celebrate Liberia's rich football culture. Supporting the community that cheers us on is as important as winning on the field.
